About this concept

niadaily is a concept by Leo Zhao: an everyday health app with an AI that knows your blood results and your daily habits. Example member, made up results and simulated watch data. Not medical advice.

Questions

How does the AI know my health?

It reads your blood results, the steps, sleep and heart rate from your phone or watch, and your daily check ins. Every action it suggests comes from a reviewed library.

Does it work with my watch?

It's built to read Apple Health, Health Connect on Android, Fitbit, Garmin and Oura. In this concept the connection and the data are simulated.

I already have a family doctor. Why would I need this?

Keep your doctor. A yearly checkup is built to catch disease, so it usually skips markers like ApoB, Lp(a) and fasting insulin that show risk years earlier. The free Check tab shows what your last test left out.

How do I know my progress is real?

Only changes bigger than normal test to test variation count as improvement, so what you see isn't noise from the lab or a recent cold.

Is this medical advice?

No. Anything that needs a clinician goes to your nurse practitioner.
